## What the player plays with

**The price lever (#221).** A price tag with a lever: push it up or down by a percent, and it shows the multiplier and the new price.

Tasks: push 80 scrap up 15% and read the multiplier (1.15). Pull it down 15% and find the multiplier (0.85, not 0.15). Run up 10% and then down 10% on 200 and watch it stop at 198.

A slip costs something small: the trader takes your coin with a grin, or the jerry can's price sticks on the wrong number and someone haggles you down.
