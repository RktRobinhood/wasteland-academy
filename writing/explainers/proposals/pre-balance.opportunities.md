- **Lines to keep word for word:**

## What the player plays with

**The scrap scales (#221): do the same to both pans.** This script is already written around the scales, so the toy only makes what Tess says into something the player does.

Tasks: take the 4 loose bolts off one pan and watch it tip. Then take 4 off the other pan too and watch it level. Split both pans three ways. In example 2, take two bags off each side.

A slip costs something small: the pan tips, bolts roll under the bench, and maybe the dog barks.

For the bracket slip, the scales need crates: a crate is a bag with 2 bolts taken out, and 4 crates sit on a pan. That shows why the 4 multiplies both the x and the −2. It's a catalogue note for #221.
