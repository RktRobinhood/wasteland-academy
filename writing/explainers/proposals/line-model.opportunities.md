## What the player plays with

**The mast and a pinned line (#221), as the airtime meter.** The line starts at the opening fee on the cost axis and climbs at the rate. The player sets the start and the rate, then runs the clock.

Tasks: run the meter minute by minute and fill in the bill (8, 11, 14, 17). Set the line so ten minutes costs 38. Drain the generator to 16 hours and keep going: the line goes below zero and the generator coughs and stops.

A slip costs something small: the trader's call cuts out mid-sentence, or the generator sputters.

This is the same toy as `gradient` and `line-equation`, with the axes labelled in context. It is the only place the catalogue's line toy has to show units.
