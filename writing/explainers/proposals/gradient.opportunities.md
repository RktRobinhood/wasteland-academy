## The opening call

The cold open already asks it: east climbs 12 over 40, west climbs 9 over 25, so which is gentler? It's the best cold-open question of the 11, because most players will get it wrong (west, since 9 < 12), and the recap pays it off.

It is a choice between two tracks, not a number, so it needs a tapped call in place of a typed or dialled one. See `opportunity-pass.md`.

- East: right. The recap's bakkie line lands.
- West: compared the climbs and not the climb per metre. The recap says nine is less than twelve, and it's still the steeper track.

## What the player plays with

**The mast and a pinned line (#221): drag a point, swing the line.** Here the line is a track up the hill, with rise and run shown as the player drags.

Tasks: drag the top of the east track and watch the rise per metre stay at 0.3 anywhere along it. Make a line with gradient 2, then −3, then 0. Drag a point onto the line through P and Q (example 3's T).

A slip costs something small: the bakkie stalls halfway up, rolls back, and oupa shouts from the back.
