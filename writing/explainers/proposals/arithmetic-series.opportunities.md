## What the player plays with

**Seat rows and the ledger column (#221)**, the same toy as `arithmetic-sequence`, with one new move: flip a copy of the column and lay it next to the first. Every pair of rows then adds to the same total.

Tasks: flip 1 to 10 under 10 to 1 and see each column make 11. Flip the pit's rows 1 to 4 (22 + 34, 26 + 30, both 56). Then the 40 rows, which only the formula can do.

This is Gauss's trick done with the player's hands, so the story can get shorter. Mags tells it while the player flips.

A slip costs something small: the column tally goes wrong, and the pit boss sells a seat that doesn't exist.
