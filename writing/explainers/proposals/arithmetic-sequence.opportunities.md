## The opening call

Row 40, how many seats? The cold open already asks it and then answers "by the end of this you'll have it". Make it the call. The player has seen rows 1 to 4 (22, 26, 30, 34) and types a number.

- 178: right.
- 182 (22 + 40 × 4): one step too many, Wal's fence. The recap can name the gap at the end of the paddock.
- 160 (40 × 4): forgot the front row's 22.
- Anything else: the recap says they'd now call 178, and why.

## What the player plays with

**Seat rows and the ledger column (#221): add a row.** The pit from the side, with the ledger beside it. Each tap adds a row of seats and writes its count in the column. The gaps between rows light up as hops.

Tasks: add rows 2 to 4 and say what each one adds (d). Count the hops from row 1 to row 4 (three, not four). Then the pit boss's problem: the toy stops at row 10 ("he's only built ten so far"), so the player can't count their way to row 40, and the formula is the way out. A send that skips the front row puts a row of seats down in the wrong place, the pit boss shouts, and Mags crosses it out in the book.

Optional gag: tap Wal's fence and watch the ute drive through the gap.

## The worked examples

- **Watch:** example 1, row 40 of the pit. Keep it and its six steps. The why-tap on 40 − 1 stays.
- **Build:** example 2, the drying well. It's the one with a negative d, so the tiles test the sign:
  - line 1: `d = 465 − 480 = −15` / `d = 480 − 465 = 15` / `d = 465`
  - line 2: `u₂₀ = 480 + (20 − 1)(−15)` / `u₂₀ = 480 + 20 × (−15)` / `u₂₀ = 480 + (20 − 1) × 15`
  - line 3: `= 480 − 285 = 195` / `= 480 + 285 = 765` / `= 480 − 300 = 180`
  - The check (195 < 480, the well is drying) stays.
- **Exam layout:** example 3, the sandbags, parts (a) to (c). Part (c) becomes a typed answer (28). The n = 1 check tap stays.

## The long run of talk

It runs from the recall tap through the big idea and into example 1's first tap, about 290 words. Mags explains the pit, the sequence, terms, uₙ, d, the hops, Wal's fence, the formula and the formula book, all without a stop.

How to break it up:
- Add rows on the toy before Mags says "four more than the one in front" (the player finds d).
- Count the hops on the toy before the fence story, so the story explains what they just saw.
- A tap: is the small number in u₄ the row or the seats? (This is the third misconception, which nothing in the current script asks about.)
- The formula book line can go, if the cut needs it. It's the last line before example 1, and the recap carries the formula.

The recall section has two versions (watched and not), and the not-watched one is 50 words of talk. That is a format question for #216, see `opportunity-pass.md`.
