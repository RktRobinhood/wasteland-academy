## What must survive

- **Stories and tics.** The counting drum and the trader on the chilly bin, waiting for an apology from a drum. The washer left on the bench. "Bits on the bench", "Sweet as", "Choice". Tess's yard: "If it's broken, it ends up in my yard."
- **Tie-ins.** It leads to `pre-balance` (the recap's "run it the other way") and to Rook's relay. The exemplar opens on (−3)² − 4 and its recap names the −13 call, which is the same slip. A rework should keep those two lessons pointing at each other.
- **Misconceptions.** Squaring without brackets (−4² = −16), multiplying before squaring, dropping the sign. The check "a square is never negative" turns up three times, and it is the takeaway.
- **Lines to keep word for word:**
  - "If it's broken, it ends up in my yard, and most of it goes back out the gate working. Most of it."
  - "He's out front on my chilly bin, waiting for an apology. From a drum."
  - "I fitted a bolt once and left the washer on the bench. Held fine, right up until the panel rattled off halfway out the gate."
  - "The minus sign is the washer. It goes in with the number, every time, and the brackets hold them together."
  - "Both right, for different questions."
  - "I stuck a pair of brackets on the side and charged him for the repair. Best money I've made all week."

## The opening call

The drum is the call. Before anything is taught, the player types what minus three squared is.

- 9: the recap says they took the trader's side, and the brackets prove them right.
- −9: they took the drum's side, which is also right, for the question with no brackets.
- Anything else: the recap gives them both answers and says which question each one belongs to.

This call has two right answers, so the recap needs a line for each. The exemplar's pattern has one matching value. See the format note in `opportunity-pass.md`.

## What the player plays with

**The counting drum: the relay (#217) as Tess's drum, with a brackets clip.** The player dials a number into a slot, sets the clip on or off, and cranks the handle. With the clip on, −3 into x² gives 9. With it off, it gives −9. That flip is the whole lesson, and the player finds it before Tess explains it.

Tasks: crank −3 with the clip off and then on (the drum's reading, then the trader's). Find two inputs that give 9 with the clip on (3 and −3). Try to get a negative out of x² with the clip on (nothing works, because a square is never negative, and that's the check the lesson leans on).

A slip costs something small: the drum jams, a washer pings off across the yard, and Tess goes to find it.

This isn't on the #221 list. It doesn't need a new toy if the relay takes a "brackets clip" option. See the catalogue note in `opportunity-pass.md`.

## The worked examples

- **Watch:** example 1, x² + 5 at x = −3. Keep it as it is.
- **Build:** example 2, 2x² − 5x at x = −2. Each wrong tile is keyed to one misconception:
  - line 1: `2(−2)² − 5(−2)` / `(2 × −2)² − 5(−2)` (multiplies before squaring) / `2 × 2² − 5 × 2` (drops the sign)
  - line 2: `= 8 + 10` / `= 16 + 10` / `= 8 − 10`
  - line 3: `= 18` / `= 26` / `= −2`
- **Exam layout:** example 3, f(x) = 4 + 2x − x². Part (b), f(−3) = −11, becomes a typed answer. The tap on why −(−3)² is −9 stays.

## The long run of talk

It runs from the recall tap through the big idea to the first tap in example 1, about 230 words. Tess explains the slot, the brackets, the washer, the no-brackets reading and the order of operations, all without a stop.

How to break it up:
- Crank the drum twice, with the clip on and off, before Tess names either reading (that replaces about 60 words).
- Tap the washer on the bench to hear the panel rattle off (optional gag, like oupa's radio in the exemplar).
- Before Tess says it, a tap: in −3², which comes first, the power or the minus?
- The order-of-operations line goes on the board as she says it, and the recap repeats it, so the big idea can drop it.

The recall tap (a negative times a negative) has no prerequisite lesson behind it. It could become the first crank instead of a separate section.
