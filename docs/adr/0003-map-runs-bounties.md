# ADR-0003: One open world, finishable adventures, durable runs and active bounties

- Status: **Accepted principles; milestone balancing OPEN**, 2026-11-21

## Context

The map and dungeon currently open all five zones and allow visiting built regions. The owner wants coherent narrative achievements, meaningful but minimal prerequisites, persistent one-at-a-time runs, optional learning supports and replayability without restricting free exploration.

## Decision

1. Keep **one canonical overworld** and preferably the same regional art/maps across all four courses. Each location retains its thematic mathematical identity. Unsupported locations remain visible scenery but are **non-interactive**; do not present out-of-syllabus content or pop-ups advertising it.
2. **All five syllabus zones are open immediately**, with **no cross-zone prerequisites**. A capable or curious student can jump into hard content, fail and learn. Within a zone only, genuinely necessary mathematical prerequisites may lock particular adventures; show the chain and exact skill requirement visibly on that zone's map.
3. Any accepted prerequisite may be satisfied by skill mastery demonstrated in **any** mode. Unlocks are permanent even if later skill mastery decreases. A short, harder **Test-Out** boss challenge exists only for an actual in-zone gate, allows immediate retries with varied questions, can unlock the route without completing the narrative boss, and suggests practice if failed. Prior evidence can unlock automatically.
4. **Region completion** is a permanent narrative achievement secured by defeating a region's boss. At that milestone, offer a choice to finish the run and collect results or keep playing in a non-narrative endless continuation for personal bests. A subsequent death does not revoke the victory or earned rewards.
5. **One active run per player**, with one selected character fixed until run end. Closing an app pauses/saves and reopens **that exact run**; no involuntary end on closing. The active run must preserve gameplay state, not only lifetime statistics.
6. **Scrap, achievements and unlocked geography persist** after death. There is no current loss-on-death, extraction or banking mechanic. Future balancing is not permission to add one now.
7. **Bounties are temporary skill-specific awareness/incentive flags**, generated by repeated struggle anywhere in the game, displayed at the *relevant maths place*. They do not create a new mode or force a special sequence of fights. The active-only Bounty Board is reachable **between runs** from the map/profile, with quick travel and 'Know Your Enemy' links to relevant explainers. A bounty clears only after meaningful improved performance and narrative progress during normal play in that location; it may reappear months later if the skill becomes a weakness again.
8. Feedback on repeated struggle is delivered in-universe at the **end-of-run debrief** (Scrap, results, active bounties, explainer links), not distracting mid-combat popups.
9. Regional high scores are **personal bests**, not global/class rankings. Show the record at entry and celebrate a new PB with an arcade-style event. Share scores across courses only when question pools and scoring are truly comparable; segment records when they are not. Score reflects progress/difficulty and accuracy, with lower bonuses for mathematical assists, not faster button presses or purchased combat power.

## Explicitly unresolved

- Exact list of within-zone prerequisite gates, skill thresholds, story boss node per region, and what degree of success counts as bounty resolution.
- Whether 'finish run' or 'continue after boss' is implemented as a single saveable state transition (recommended, but code verification required).
- Formula for regional score and comparison keys when some courses have different question pools.

## Verification

- Each course can view the common map; only eligible locations can receive interaction.
- Every zone can be entered from a brand-new save; cross-zone mastery never gates it.
- In-zone prerequisites unlock from any mode and never relock. Passing Test-Out does not mark region boss beaten.
- Run save/restore matches HP, question generation/seed, character, inventory, perks, timer policy, progression, difficulty, rewards and earned milestone.
- Region victory persisted before optional endless continuation; death cannot undo it.
- Active bounties only, and repeat bounties after later decline; no board in active run; shortcuts work between runs.
