## Transparent cut revision

Background extraction, precise. Keep only the exact drawn amber glassback sand lizard, fully unchanged (body, hooked sandy tail, claws, eye and quartz fin colours). REMOVE the pale translucent halo/white-grey cloud that sits BEHIND and around the silhouette entirely. The transparent empty area needs alpha exactly zero right up to the actual lizard body outline, fin edges, claws and tail. Preserve solid amber crystal plates with the actual normalized polygon outline, don't remove the lizard's pale markings or make holes in its body. Maintain left-facing side view, size, relative pose, contours and expression, no framing crop at nose and tail, no white strokes, no glow, no color spill, no ground, no canvas, no shadow, no texture outside the creature, no other subject; true transparent PNG. For an authentic game sprite, hard clean outer alpha with a small antialiased edge only.

The initial render had a visible background halo; it was rejected. The accepted revision is the only master delivered.
