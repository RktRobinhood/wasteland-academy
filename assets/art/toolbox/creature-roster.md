# Regional creature roster

**Target: 35 distinct designs across five existing sectors.** The working art labels are not narrative canon or encounter rules. Count silhouettes, not colourways, props, bosses, or animation frames.

| Sector | Visual vocabulary | Seven proposed families |
|---|---|---|
| Scrap Fields | Riveted copper, patched tires, low squat forms | **Copper Mite** (shelf), Jackstand Crab, Axle Hound, Cable Rat, Treadback, Kettleback Toad, Pulley Wasp (flyer) |
| Signal Tower | Weathered relay cases, brass, narrow aerials | Perch Receiver (flyer), Insulator Gecko, Ladder Centipede, Relay Moth (flyer), Bellwire Lynx, Switchboard Spider, Shard Crow (flyer) |
| Dry Flats | Sun-bleached scales, angular outlines, canvas vanes | Salt Sifter, Dune Cutwing (flyer), Windvane Skitter, **Glassback Lizard** (shelf), Dust Hare, Sunplate Beetle, Canvas Ray (flyer) |
| Radio Spires | Insulated cables, slate equipment and odd aerial shapes | **Antenna Kite** (shelf), Cable-Slung Slug, Ferrite Bat (flyer), Static Tern (flyer), Fork Relay, Coil Viper, Linewalker |
| Engine Core | Sooty heavy plating, ceramic heat shields, ember amber | **Boiler Crawler** (shelf), Cinderback Salamander, Flywheel Tick, Heatshield Pillbug, Vent Hoverer (flyer), Steam Hermit, Soot Finch (flyer) |

**4/35 new families with shelf assets, 0/35 integrated.** Native Dishback (Signal Tower) and the shared D-tier roster are not counted. Mite has four held poses, no walk. Kite is an articulated idle/dodge proof with a still, not full combat coverage. Lizard and Crawler each have one ambient static frame, not combat. The other labels are art briefs only. Keep a deliberate mix of mechanical, wild, and biomechanical silhouettes by sector.
