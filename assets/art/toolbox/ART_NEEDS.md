## Requests

Add a new entry when a specific gap is observed. Suggested information:

- **Asset and use**: name, scene, biome (or shared human encounter), and current missing/weak asset path.
- **Needs**: orientation, framing, scale/anchor, motion states, day/night needs and native import format.
- **Reference**: exact existing asset/scene, screenshot or revision; mention any protected hit areas, authored art or states to preserve.
- **Status**: requested / ready on art shelf / selected for integration / integrated. Record the art path and exact branch commit when ready; record the gameplay PR separately when integrated.
